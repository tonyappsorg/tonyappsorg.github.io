Start with the foundation

One company, the right access for every buyer

A business can register on the storefront, or your team can create its account in the admin. New storefront registrations wait for approval. Once approved, the company administrator can add colleagues from My Company and give them roles that control what they may see and do.

01

Company structure

Create company accounts, users and branches. Give teams access to company orders according to their roles.

02

Company pricing

Assign a customer group to a company, then use Magento's tier prices and price rules for its members.

03

Payment control

Choose allowed payment methods by company and offer a shared store-credit balance to eligible members.

How company buying works

From registration to checkout

  1. 01

    Create and approve

    The business registers, or your team creates the company. Storefront registrations wait for approval.

  2. 02

    Add users and roles

    The company administrator adds colleagues and chooses what each role can manage or buy.

  3. 03

    Set company terms

    Assign a pricing group, choose available payment methods and, if useful, add store credit.

  4. 04

    Buy in Magento

    Members place orders through the normal checkout with their company's prices and payment rules.

Built around Magento

Company terms, normal checkout

Company pricing uses Magento customer groups and existing price rules. Allowed payment methods are checked for the company. Shared store credit appears when the balance covers the whole order and the member can spend it.

Quick Order and Requisition Lists add products to the regular cart. Quotes and approvals add the steps your business needs before the buyer completes checkout.

Good to know

Common questions

Do I need all five B2B modules?

No. Company Accounts is the base. Add Quick Order, Requisition Lists, Quotes or Order Approval only when you need those workflows. Requisition Lists also requires Quick Order.

Can each company have its own prices?

Yes. Assign a customer group to the company, then use Magento's tier prices, catalog price rules and cart price rules for that group.

Can a buyer use a quote and order approval together?

Yes. A quoted cart above the approval threshold still needs the company administrator's approval before checkout.

Does store credit support split payment?

No. Store credit covers the whole order or is not offered as a payment method for that order.

Does this work with Hyvä?

The company account pages and add-on customer pages support Luma and Hyvä. Checkout uses your store's configured integration; Hyvä Checkout has not been verified for this suite.

Can I use it with Adobe Commerce B2B?

No. These modules are built for Magento Open Source. They do not run alongside Adobe Commerce B2B modules and do not provide a migration path to them.
