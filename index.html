Pick a package, check its requirements and installation notes, then add it to your Magento project with Composer.

Find Tonyapps on Packagist ↗
FROM YOUR MAGENTO PROJECT
$composer require tonyapps/module-name
$bin/magento setup:upgrade

Replace module-name with the package name. Check its README for requirements, setup, and configuration.
